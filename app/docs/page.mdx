## POTHOS/UI

Build stunning, modern portfolios with a flexible UI kit and component library.<br />
Simple. Elegant. Fully customizable.
Designed for creators, developers, and designers who want to ship fast without sacrificing style.

POTHOS/UI offers a collection of reusable components and layouts built on top of **shadcn/ui**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **Framer Motion**. Craft beautiful, responsive portfolios that feel unique and professional—without reinventing the wheel.

### Vision

I wanted a UI library that felt modern and creative, but still practical for real-world projects. **POTHOS/UI** is designed to fill that gap—helping you build polished portfolios that stand out.

#### Easy to use and customize

Every component comes with clear examples and is reusable out of the box. You can drop them in as-is or tailor them to match your brand, design system, or project requirements.

#### Built on shadcn/ui, Tailwind CSS, and Framer Motion

POTHOS/UI takes advantage of **shadcn/ui’s accessibility-first design**, **Tailwind’s utility classes**, and **Framer Motion’s smooth, intuitive animations** to give you power, flexibility, and motion out of the box. It’s opinionated enough to look good immediately, but open enough to adapt to your style.

#### Open source and community-driven

POTHOS/UI is [open-source](https://github.com/BounaderMedRafik/pothos).
Contributions and feedback are welcome to grow the collection and make it even better.
